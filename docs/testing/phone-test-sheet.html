Cricket Replay: two-phone test sheet

You need one iPhone and one OnePlus. You do not need to understand software. Follow the numbered steps, then record what actually happened. If something is confusing or a button is missing, choose Could not test and explain why.

Before handing this to the tester: the app owner must install the intended test app on both phones and check that both have a Rolling camera recording section. The iPhone recording build has not yet been installed or physically verified. Write the build identifiers below; an older app may not have these buttons. Browser previews cannot record.

What to prepare

  • Allow about 90–120 minutes. Test recording on one phone at a time, including 30 uninterrupted minutes on each.
  • Charge both phones beforehand. Note whether they are plugged in during testing. Prop the recording phone sideways, with its rear camera pointing at the other phone's stopwatch and a moving object. A clock with seconds also works. Avoid filming people or private information.
  • Use the other phone's stopwatch as the timer in the picture. Occasionally move a hand or ball beside it. Keep that stopwatch visible and running. The recording phone has no live camera preview.
  • For connection tests, both phones need the same Wi-Fi network. Internet is not needed to record or play a clip. Do not change router settings unless its owner has agreed.
  • Keep the recording app on screen during recording. Do not switch to this form, Notes or Settings on that phone mid-run. Fill the sheet on paper, or on the other phone after a checkpoint; keep its stopwatch visible when filming. Do not use screen recording, which could change performance.
  • Capture clips have no sound. A silent clip is expected. The app is an experiment; Extract latest review clip means “make a short video of what just happened.”

How to record results

For every test choose As expected, Problem seen, or Could not test. Describe any freeze, skipped movement, app closing, unexpected stop or message. Copy error messages exactly, or photograph just the message. Never include the pairing QR code, secret, or personal video in what you send back.

Writing “As expected” describes what you observed; the app owner must still review the measurements. Do not guess numbers. Use “not shown” if a number is absent. Starting a new recording deletes the old recording and clip: save its report first.

Two-phone connection tests

Stop recording on both phones first. These tests send a generated demonstration video, not the camera clip you recorded. Camera clips currently stay on the phone that recorded them.

Join both phones to the same Wi-Fi. If asked on iPhone, allow Local Network. Keep both apps on screen. “Host” is the phone receiving the demonstration video; “Camera” is the phone sending it.

P3 — Interrupted send and retry

  1. Repeat P1 steps 1–2 to pair again. On the Camera phone generate a sample if none is ready, then tick Slow transfer for interruption test.
  2. Tap Send sample to host. While progress is between the start and finish, tap Stop session.
  3. Check that the interrupted video is not offered as a newly playable completed video. If an earlier successful video is still shown, write that down; it is not proof the interrupted send completed.
  4. Tap Stop session on both phones. Start a fresh host QR and scan it again. Untick slow transfer, send again and play on the Host.

Expected: interruption is clearly shown; reconnecting and resending produces a verified, playable demonstration video.

Optional network description

Write whether you used home Wi-Fi or a phone hotspot, which phone supplied the hotspot, and whether internet was available. A home Wi-Fi pass does not establish that every hotspot setup works. Leave hotspot testing to the owner unless it has been set up for you.

What to send back

  1. Save this completed sheet using Save results file or PDF. A completed paper copy photographed clearly is also fine.
  2. Include each phone's copied experiment reports, labeled with phone and test name. Keep reports locally in Notes or a text file if sending must wait.
  3. Include exact error text and observations. Do not send footage, pairing codes or passwords.

Do not start another run or tap Delete recording experiment until you have saved its report. Once the owner confirms the evidence is saved, stop recording, close video playback, and delete the temporary experiment and samples.
