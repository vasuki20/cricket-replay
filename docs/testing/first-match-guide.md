# First match: two phones

**iPhone 15 Pro camera → OnePlus host; iPhone 15 viewer. One More Look, build 17 (0.1.1).**

1. Turn mobile data off on both phones. Enable the OnePlus hotspot and join it from the iPhone. If the hotspot needs mobile data, record the problem; a router test does not prove field readiness.
2. OnePlus: **Host** opens **Camera QR**. iPhone: **Camera** opens the scanner. Allow camera/local-network permissions. Pairing starts capture automatically.
3. Hold the camera sideways before pairing starts recording; if you turn it after pairing, tap Stop, place it, then Record for a fresh landscape capture. Early portrait footage remains fitted inside a landscape recording canvas, which makes its picture appear smaller. Position the iPhone using the full-screen preview. Keep the phone fixed and the app open. **Record / Stop** overlay the full-screen preview. Tap the picture to hide/show controls; they hide after 3.5 seconds. Camera guides have been removed.
4. On OnePlus, tap **Review last 20 s**. Wait for the recorded window to fill. Progress opens full screen, then video opens automatically. Tap the picture to show/hide overlay controls; they hide after 3.5 seconds. Switch **Video / Frames** inside full-screen review. Main pages stay portrait; camera and replay screens rotate. Video: Play/Pause, seek, speed, Fit/Fill and Close. Frames: slider for quick positioning, **Previous / Next** for precise steps, **1× / 2× / 3×** zoom, **Video** to switch to playback, and **Close** to return to Host. Fit preserves the full picture; Fill crops the edges. Host **Settings** displays the camera’s current review/buffer durations; camera settings are absent from Match mode.
5. **Save** keeps an app-local copy and opens sharing. Saved copies survive End match; uninstalling removes app-local files. After Wi-Fi loss, rejoin the hotspot and wait for automatic reconnection. A socket disconnect leaves recording running. Locking/backgrounding the camera stops capture; return and press **Record**. If pairing changes, stop and scan the new QR.
6. Extra iPhone: choose **Viewer**, scan the host’s **Viewer QR**, and tap **Latest replay** after the host has reviewed footage. Viewers download only the latest completed clip; each controls Video/Frames independently. Tap again to fetch a newer clip. **No replay yet** means the host has not completed a review. Use **Leave** to disconnect the viewer without ending the match. Keep all apps open on the same Wi-Fi/hotspot. Trial limit: one camera and four viewers; no replay history or viewer-triggered camera extraction.
7. **End match** stops recording and deletes temporary footage. If the other phone did not acknowledge, end it there too. Close playback before ending.

Rehearsal: record for **30 minutes**, request **three real clips**, check motion around segment joins and confirm capture continues. Measure setup after Wi-Fi association (target <3 minutes) and Review tap to playback (target ≤30 seconds). Check portrait/landscape layout, camera/replay overlay hiding and revealing without resizing footage, both review modes, viewer joining/downloading while the host plays, independent playback, viewer Leave, slider scrubbing followed by precise steps, reconnection, lock recovery, saved exports and End match.

Build 7 corrected iPhone capture orientation; validate with newly recorded footage. Keep the camera sideways for a landscape view. Build 8 requires new physical layout/frame-review checks. Synthetic tests and successful builds do not establish camera angle, recording endurance or match readiness. Follow the [readiness record](first-match-readiness.md) before the field trial.

Build 17 sends replay chunks in bounded bursts and avoids a fixed clock-calibration wait to reduce network waiting. For the latency check, record at least 25 seconds, time Review → playback, then close playback and read Diagnostics → Camera-to-host review: Transfer / Verify. Record these separately from total tap-to-play time. Both the camera and host need build 17 for the faster path; older peers remain compatible. Viewer installation status is recorded in [readiness](first-match-readiness.md).

During host/viewer review, Android Back closes fullscreen review and returns to the match. Home/return retains the video position and play/pause state or the current frame while the app process survives. Camera recording still stops in background; return and press Record. Force-stop or OS process eviction does not yet restore the screen automatically.
